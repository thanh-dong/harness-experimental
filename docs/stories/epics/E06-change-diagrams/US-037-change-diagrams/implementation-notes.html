deviation Deviations

  • Intake ran without the griller's conversational checkpoint: the diagram set, format, and review mechanism were already decided in the preceding analysis turns, and the user's "update the harness to have them" is the explicit confirmation docs/HARNESS.md requires before replacing the feature workflow.
  • No D1 for this story: the impact-analysis capability is inactive on this clone (no provider registered), which per the new table means D1 is not required on normal lane. Noted in the intake row.

tradeoff Tradeoffs & accepted limits

  • The lint checks structure only; "diagram matches code" remains a human/second-agent done-gate judgement. A future story verify hook could require reviewed status for required diagrams.
  • Two places record a review (file header + intervention). Accepted for readability; the rule states the intervention wins on disagreement.
  • Hand-drawn kinds (D3–D7) have no freshness generator; drift is handled by the stale rule and story signals, not by tooling.
